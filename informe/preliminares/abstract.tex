\preliminar{Abstract}

\noindent
This project addresses the inefficiency of the package tour sales and
booking process at a travel agency in the city of Potosí, where seat
availability checks and information updates are not handled uniformly among
staff members. The general objective is to develop a web system that
manages tour packages, departure dates and bookings, validates seat
availability and improves trip confirmation. The proposal is based on the
background of reservation systems, the management of tour packages and
usability and security criteria for web applications. The methodology
includes interviews, observation and authorized document review at the
selected agency in order to diagnose the current process and design a
solution that meets its needs. The system is expected to help reduce
availability inconsistencies, shorten confirmation times and provide
reliable information for decision making.

\vspace{0.5\baselineskip}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} travel agencies, tour packages, bookings, web
systems, availability control
